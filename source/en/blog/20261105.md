## Sparkles Again, and This Time It's Beta

If the holographic card just floated onto your screen, you're right: this is our second **shiny release**. **Version 1.0.0-beta.1** closes the alpha. From now on, pages and navigation keep their shape, and the work goes into polish on the road to 1.0.

Five weeks went into it, so here is the short version. The biggest piece, **We-Fly in French, German, Dutch, Italian and Spanish**, has [its own post](/blog/20261013), with how to pick your language and how to help us polish the translations.

## Can I Fly Here Now?

- **A redesigned takeoff page.** It opens on a **Can I fly now?** card: the site's wind sector on a compass, the live wind from the nearest station and a plain verdict (Flyable, Marginal, Not flyable) with its reason. Site rules and warnings from the community sit in a **Before you fly here** callout. The rest is split into **Overview**, **Site**, **Activity** and **Wind flow** tabs.
- **Wind flow in 3D.** Pick a wind direction and strength, press **Simulate the airflow** and watch the air speed up over the ridge, rise, sink and roll into rotors in the lee. A learning aid to understand a site, not a forecast. Like the relief? Press **Download STL** to save it as a 3D printable model, 100, 150 or 200 mm across, ready for your slicer.
- **Launch forecast on the Takeoffs map.** One tap colours every takeoff good, marginal or unsuitable for the next five days, with a timeline to drag or play. Right-click (or long-press) anywhere for a **meteogram**.
- **About a thousand takeoffs** created from pilots' flights are back on the map, the planner and the forecast.

## A Smarter XC Planner

- **Hour-by-hour launch verdicts** in the first step, with the same five-day timeline and a meteogram anywhere on the map. The briefing now picks the **best free weather model** for your takeoff (AROME, ICON-CH, the Met Office model, HRRR and more).
- **Live traffic** from the Open Glider Network: paragliders, hang gliders and gliders each drawn with their own shape. Tap one to see its card and today's trace. Anonymous pilots stay anonymous.
- **Reach in the 3D review**: see the ground you can still glide to from any point, using a glide ratio you type or the estimate from one of your own wings.
- **Save your airspace file** to your account, ready for your connected instrument.
- The planner now **starts on today's date** and tells you what is missing before **Continue**.

## Know Your Wing

On the **Wings** page, expand a wing to see its **glide performance**: airspeed, sink and glide ratio at trim and on bar, worked out from your own straight glides. Then:

- **compare two to four of your wings** on one chart,
- **compare with other pilots** flying the same wing,
- **compare any wings** across the community from the leaderboard,
- and give each wing **its own colour** in the logbook.

## More From Every Flight

- **Your flight in 3D, first thing.** The Overview map now opens on a **3D** view: the terrain under your flight, your track coloured by climb rate and each thermal as a plume. Press play on the bar beneath it to **replay the flight**: a dot flies your track with a line down to the ground, the trace draws itself behind it, and the slider over your altitude profile jumps to any moment, at 10× to 300×.
- **Measure any stretch**: drag across the altitude, vario, speed or G-force chart for its duration, distance, speeds, height gained and average vario.
- **New numbers** on the Summary: a **Heights** card, time spent climbing, best straight glide, fastest 25, 50 and 100 km, and a **data quality** note when a file is patchy. The Cockpit gets matching **personal bests** and a **Climb by hour of day** chart.
- **Shared thermals**: the same-day comparison lists every climb where another pilot was in your thermal, and how you climbed against them, in a prettier 3D thermal view.
- **Trends** in the Cockpit show how your flying changes by day, week, month or year.
- **Vector Vario** data now shows up even when a paired flight app wrote the file, its measured wind feeds the **Wind** tab, and heart rate gets its own tab.

## Flights In, Flights Out

- **PGPilot**: connect it under **Profile → Connected services** and your flights land in your logbook on their own.
- **Online leagues**: send flights to the **CFD** and **DHV-XC** from a flight's menu, or automatically on every upload.
- **Connected apps** now see every task on your account.

## Honest Numbers and Quiet Fixes

- **Your IGC files are never changed.** Wing, date and name corrections live in your logbook only, and accents in the file header are kept. The file you download is the one your instrument wrote.
- **One altitude reference** for maximum altitude, ceiling, thermal tops and heights above ground, matching your vario. Curves no longer dip under the ground on low sites on a high-pressure day.
- **Max gain** counts only height you actually climbed. A sled ride is no longer a 1,500 m gain.
- **One bad timestamp** no longer turns a short flight into a 14-hour one, and flights crossing midnight UTC land on the right day.
- **Re-uploading** an older flight no longer stores it twice.
- **Stay signed in** for 30 days between visits instead of 24 hours.
- Your **logbook totals** add up every matching flight again, pages open on **older browsers**, and the **Thermal Strength** badge matches the max climb on your flight.
- A **new We-Fly logo**, with the pilot under the wing, everywhere from the top bar to your home screen icon.
- A **Tips to get started** switch in your profile, and a one-click unsubscribe in every tip email.

The full list, with every detail, is in the release notes on the [About](/about) page.
