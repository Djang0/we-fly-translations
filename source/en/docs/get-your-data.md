## Your data, your rules

Head over to **/profile** and you'll find a shiny "Download Your Data" button. One click and boom, a ZIP file lands in your downloads faster than a sledge ride after a blown launch.

## What's in the box?

The archive contains everything we have on you (GDPR style):

- **All your IGC files**: organized by date (`flights/YYYY/MM/DD/`) so you can find that epic thermal day from 2021
- **data-export.json**, the nerdy stuff: flight metadata, distances, durations, takeoff coords, your profile info, consent records
- **README.txt**: explains what's what in case you open it 6 beers later

## Why bother?

- **Backup**: Because hard drives fail but cloud thermals don't
- **Migrate**: Taking your logbook to another platform? Here's everything in standard formats
- **Proof**: Need to show your club you actually flew that triangle? It's all there

## Tech bits for the curious

IGC files work with any flight analysis software: XCTrack, SeeYou, whatever your crew uses. The JSON is structured and machine-readable, so you can script your own stats if you're that kind of pilot.

Fair warning: the export is rate-limited, so maybe don't spam the button like you're clicking through a pre-flight checklist.
