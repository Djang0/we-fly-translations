## The Filter Panel

Found in the sidebar, this little gem lets you slice and dice your flight data like a thermal slicer on a glorious spring day.

**What you can filter:**
- **Wing**: Because we all know that EN-B from 2019 doesn't fly like your shiny new 2-liner
- **Season**: Compare your summer XC adventures vs those sketchy winter sledders
- **Countries**: Flex those international airtime hours
- **Takeoffs**: Your home site vs that epic trip spot
- **Flight type**: Free flights, comps, SIV sessions... you name it
- **Date range**: Dial in that specific period when you were "in the zone"

## Where it matters

These filters apply to both **/flights** (your logbook) and **/cockpit** (your stats dashboard). Set a filter once, and watch both views update. No need to configure twice.

## Pro tip

Hit the reset button when you want the full picture back. We've all been there, wondering why the stats look weird, only to realize we left "Slovenia 2022" filtered from three beers ago.
