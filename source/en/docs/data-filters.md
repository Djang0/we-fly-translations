## The Filter Panel

Open **Filters** above your logbook or the Cockpit, and this little gem lets you slice and dice your flight data like a thermal slicer on a glorious spring day.

**What you can filter:**
- **Type**: Free flight, flat triangle, FAI triangle and their closed versions
- **Mode**: Solo or tandem
- **Launch**: How you got into the air
- **Wing**: Because we all know that EN-B from 2019 doesn't fly like your shiny new 2-liner
- **Country**: Flex those international airtime hours
- **Season**: Compare your summer XC adventures vs those sketchy winter sledders
- **Takeoff**: Your home site vs that epic trip spot (search by name, or tap **Near me**)
- **Date range**: Dial in that specific period when you were "in the zone" (or pick **This year** or **Last 30 days**)
- **Min distance**: Only the flights that went somewhere
- **Thermals per flight**: Separate the climbing days from the sled rides

Found a combination you keep coming back to? Save it as a preset with **Save current…** and apply it in one click next time.

## Where it matters

These filters apply to both **Logbook** and **Cockpit** (your stats dashboard). Set a filter once, and watch both views update. No need to configure twice.

## Pro tip

Hit **Clear all** when you want the full picture back. We've all been there, wondering why the stats look weird, only to realize we left "Slovenia 2022" filtered from three beers ago.
