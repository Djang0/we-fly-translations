## 4. Your content

"Your content" means the IGC files, flights, flight comments, wings, licences, tasks and any other data you add to We-Fly.

- **You keep ownership.** Uploading a flight does not transfer any rights to us.
- **What you let us do with it.** You give us the permission we need to run the service: store your content, process it (scoring, thermal analysis, statistics), and show it to the people you choose. If you share a flight, a replay or your profile, or set your profile to public, it is shown to those people and nobody else. This permission is non-exclusive and ends when you delete the content or your account, apart from backups that expire on their normal schedule (see the [Data Retention Policy](/doc/data-retention-policy)).
- **Community statistics.** Pages such as community stats, site rankings and thermal hotspots are built from many pilots' flights. Individual pilots only appear in them when their profile is public. Anonymous, aggregated figures (for example "flights logged at this takeoff") may stay after you leave, because they no longer identify you.
- **Your responsibility.** Only upload content you have the right to share. Don't upload other pilots' flights as your own.

## 7. Flight safety

Paragliding is dangerous. We-Fly is an analysis tool, **not a flight instrument and not a source of official aeronautical information**.

- Airspace, NOTAMs, weather, terrain, wind and thermal information may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always check official sources before you fly.
- Statistics, scores, badges and recommendations describe past flights. They say nothing about whether conditions are safe today.
- Live tracking and safety notifications can fail (no signal, flat battery, server issues). Never make them your only safety measure.

You alone are responsible for your decisions in the air.
