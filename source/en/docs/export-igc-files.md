## Your flights are already out there: let's bring them home

Already logging on XContest, DHV-XC, or have years of tracks sitting on your Syride? No need to re-fly everything. Here's how to yank those IGC files out of wherever they're hiding and get them into We-Fly.

---

## Online Platforms

### XContest

The world's biggest free flight logbook. Your tracks are there. You just need to dig them out.

1. Log in at [xcontest.org](https://xcontest.org)
2. Click your username → **My flights**
3. Open any flight from the list
4. On the flight detail page, look for the **download icon** ("IGC file" label) near the track map
5. Click it. Your browser grabs the `.igc` file

Bulk downloads are also available directly:
- Go to [xcontest.org](https://xcontest.org)
- Navigate to **My flights**
- Select a season that already has flights; 
- Click the **ALL IGC** link (bottom left)
- Download the ZIP file

---

### DHV-XC

Germany's national online contest: rock-solid records, slightly complex UI.

1. Log in at [dhv-xc.de](https://www.dhv-xc.de)
2. Navigate to **My flights** (Meine Flüge)
3. Open the flight detail page
4. Find the **IGC download link** at the bottom of the page (often labeled "Download IGC-File")
5. Done, grab the file

## Mobile Apps

### Flyskyhy (iOS)

The go-to logger for Apple pilots. Your IGC files live right in the app.

1. Open Flyskyhy → **Logbook**
2. Tap the flight you want
3. Tap the **share icon** (top right corner, the box with the arrow)
4. Select **send to XContest** or **Send to DHV-XC**
5. Then you can download the IGC file from the platform.

**Pro tip:** You can also find your IGC files via the Files app under Flyskyhy's app folder if you've enabled Files access.

---

### XCTrack (Android)

The paragliding instrument app for Android, also the OS powering instruments like the AIR³. Tracks save automatically after every flight.

**Via the Share widget (easiest):**
1. On the main screen, tap the **Share widget** (top right corner, looks like a share/upload icon)
2. Choose any installed app: Gmail, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive, etc.
3. The IGC file is attached and on its way

**Via the Tracklogs menu:**
1. Open XCTrack → **Menu** (top left) → **Tracklogs**
2. Long-press the flight or tap the three-dot menu icon
3. Select **Share** → pick your destination app
4. Send to yourself or save directly to your phone

**Via XContest auto-upload (if already connected):**
1. Link your XContest account under **Menu → Preferences → Pilot**
2. After landing with internet access, the track uploads automatically
3. Then download the IGC from XContest (see above)

**Via USB (direct file grab):**
- Files live at `/storage/emulated/0/XCTrack/tracks/` (or on your SD card)
- Connect via USB → enable **File Transfer** mode → navigate to that folder → copy the `.igc` files

---

### XCSoar (Kobo / Android / Windows)

XCSoar logs IGC files natively. No conversion needed.

1. Flights are saved to the **XCSoar/logs/** folder on your device
2. On Kobo: connect via USB → browse to `XCSoar/logs/` → copy files
3. On Android: same folder via USB file transfer or a file manager app
4. Files are named by date: `2024-07-15-XCS-XXX-01.igc`

## Instruments & Hardware

### Oudie / Naviter (Oudie N, Omni, Oudie IGC …)

Naviter makes several devices: some are dedicated loggers, some run Android. The export path depends on which one you have.

**Oudie N / Oudie Omni (Android-based, runs SeeYou Navigator):**

*Via the SeeYou Navigator app:*
1. Open **SeeYou Navigator** → tap **Menu** → **Logbook**
2. Select a flight
3. Tap **Upload** icon → pick your platform (XContest, DHV-XC, etc.)
4. Download flight from the selected platform.

*Via USB:*
1. Connect via USB → enable **File Transfer** on the device
2. Navigate to `Internal storage/SeeYou Navigator/` (or similar)
3. Copy the `.igc` files

*Via SeeYou Cloud (if auto-sync is on):*
1. Log in at [seeyou.cloud](https://seeyou.cloud)
2. Go to **Flights** → open a flight
3. Click **Download IGC**

**SeeYou Navigator app (on your own Android/iOS phone):**
- Same as Oudie N above: Logbook → select flight → send

---

### Skytraxx (2.1, 4.0, 5.0 …)

Plug-and-play. Skytraxx mounts as a USB drive: no drivers, no drama.

1. Connect your Skytraxx to your computer via USB
2. It shows up as an external drive
3. Open the drive → look for the **IGC** folder (or files in the root directory)
4. Copy the `.igc` files you want

---

### Syride (SYS series)

French-made, rock-solid instruments. Files transfer via USB or desktop software.

**USB (fastest):**
1. Connect your Syride instrument via USB, it mounts as a drive
2. Open the **FLIGHTS** folder on the device
3. Copy the `.igc` files to your computer

**Via [syride.com](https://syride.com):**
1. Log in → **My flights**
2. Click a flight → **Download IGC**

---

### Flymaster (B1 Nav, Live SD, …)

1. Connect your Flymaster via USB, mounts as a drive
2. Open the **TRACKS** or **FLIGHTS** folder
3. Copy your `.igc` files

**Via [flymaster.net](https://flymaster.net):**
1. Log in → **My flights**
2. Open a flight → **Download IGC**

---

### SkyBean (SkyDrop, SkyDrop+)

SkyDrop is a tiny, lightweight logger that records IGC files.

1. Connect SkyDrop to your computer via USB
2. Navigate to the **IGC** or **FLIGHTS** folder
3. Copy the files
