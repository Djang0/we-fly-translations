We-Fly offers multiple ways to share your flights.

## Overview

We-Fly provides two main sharing systems:

1. **Secret Links** - Share individual flights privately via unique URLs
2. **Public Profile** - Opt-in to make your flights discoverable in the community

Both systems give you control over your privacy while enabling sharing when desired.

---

## Secret Flight Sharing

### What are Secret Links?

Secret links allow you to share individual flights with anyone, without requiring them to have a We-Fly account.

### How It Works

**For you (flight owner):**
1. Open any of your flights
2. Click the **Share flight** button (the share icon in the flight's toolbar)
3. A unique URL is generated and copied to your clipboard (on a phone, the share sheet opens instead)
4. Share the link with anyone (email, message, social media)

From your logbook, the row menu's **Copy link** does the same in one go.

**For recipients:**
1. Click the shared link
2. View flight without logging in
3. See full flight analysis and map
4. No We-Fly account required


### Security Features

- **Cryptographically secure** - 43-character random ID
- **Unguessable** - (almost) Impossible to discover without the link
- **Not searchable** - Links don't appear in community or search
- **Permanent** - Links remain valid until you delete the flight
- **Revocable** - revoke the link from **Shared links** (in the Account menu), or delete the flight

### What's Visible in Secret Links

Recipients can see:
- Complete flight track on map
- XContest scoring details
- All flight statistics and analysis
- Thermal analysis
- Wind estimation
- Takeoff and landing information

Recipients CANNOT see:
- Your other flights
- Your profile information
- Your personal bests
- Other pilots' flights

### Use Cases

**When to use secret links:**
- Share specific flight with friends
- Post flight to social media
- Send to coach for feedback
- Share with competition judges
- Provide to insurance company
- Archive flight externally

**Privacy level:** Semi-private (anyone with link can view)

## Public Profile & Community

### What is a Public Profile?

Enabling your public profile makes your flights discoverable in the We-Fly community, allowing other pilots to see your activity and progress.

### Opt-In System

**Important:** Public profile is opt-in only!
- **Default** - All accounts are private
- **To enable** - Must explicitly toggle setting in profile
- **Reversible** - Can disable at any time

### How to Enable Public Profile

1. Open your **Profile** (Account menu)
2. Click **Privacy settings**
3. Switch on **Public Profile**

The change applies right away, no save button needed.

Your flights are now visible in the community!

### What Becomes Public

When you enable public profile:

**Your flights** - Visible in community flight list
**Your statistics** - Total flights, distances, etc.
**Your name** - Display name from profile
**Your logbook** - Other pilots can browse your flights
**Flight details** - Anyone can view your flight analysis

### What Remains Private

Even with public profile:
- Email address
- Account settings
- Private flight notes (if any)
- Account password

### Disabling Public Profile

To go private again:
1. Open your **Profile**
2. Click **Privacy settings**
3. Switch off **Public Profile**

**Effect:**
- Your flights disappear from community listings
- Your shared pilot page link stops working
- Your profile becomes private
- Secret flight links continue to work

## Community Features

Access community features from the **Community** area of the menu. You need to be signed in; browsing works whether your own profile is public or private.

### Community Hub Sections

#### 1. Public Flights (/community/flights)

Browse flights shared by the community:

**Features:**
- List of all public flights
- Filter by date, location, distance
- Sort by date, distance, score
- Click to view full flight analysis

**What you see:**
- Pilot name
- Flight date and location
- XC distance and score
- Flight type (free, triangle)
- Duration and max altitude

**Click any flight** to view:
- Complete GPS track on map
- Full flight statistics
- XContest scoring
- Thermal analysis

#### 2. Pilots Directory (/community/pilots)

Discover other pilots with public profiles:

**Features:**
- List of pilots with public profiles
- Search by name
- View pilot statistics
- Click to see pilot's flight history

**Pilot information shown:**
- Pilot name
- Total flights
- Total XC distance
- Best flight statistics
- Recent activity

**Click any pilot** to view:
- Complete flight logbook (if public)
- Statistics and achievements
- Flight map (recent flights)

#### 3. Takeoffs and Map Search

Explore sites with community activity:

- **Takeoff database** (/takeoffs): launch sites worldwide, with their history
- **Search flights on the map** (/community/search-flights): pick a takeoff and see every community flight launched from it

**Use cases:**
- Find active flying sites
- See what others are flying from your local sites
- Discover new destinations

#### And More

The Community menu also leads to **Following**, **Clubs**, **Leaderboard**, **Instruments**, **Place to be** and **Seasons recap**.

### Filtering & Search

- **Flights**: search box, date presets (Today, Past 7 days, Past 30 days, Past 90 days, Past year, Custom), plus Pilot, Site, Glider and Launch filters
- **Pilots**: search by name, minimum flight count, filter chips and several sort orders

## Privacy Summary

### Your Control

You have complete control over sharing:

| Feature | Who Can See | Requires Opt-In? |
|---------|-------------|------------------|
| Private flights | Only you | Default |
| Secret links | Anyone with link | Per-flight choice |
| Public profile | Everyone with a we-fly account | Yes, must enable |

### Privacy Levels

**Private (Default)**
- No one can see your flights
- Your data is completely private
- No community visibility

**Secret Link Sharing**
- Only people with link can view
- Flight is not searchable/discoverable
- Semi-private sharing

**Public Profile**
- Flights visible to all we-fly pilots
- Discoverable in community
- Profile is searchable

### Best Practices

**For maximum privacy:**
- Don't enable public profile
- Only share secret links with trusted people
- Be selective about which flights you share

**For community engagement:**
- Enable public profile
- Contribute to site database
- Connect with other pilots
- Share your achievements

## Community Guidelines

### Respectful Sharing

When sharing flights publicly:
- Share your own flights only
- Respect other pilots' privacy
- Provide accurate information
- Don't share others' private data
- Don't misrepresent flights

### Data Accuracy

Help maintain data quality:
- Upload accurate IGC files
- Ensure GPS data is valid
- Report any data issues
- Don't modify IGC files (except allowed metadata)
- Don't upload fake flights

### Safety & Responsibility

**Remember:**
- Sharing a flight doesn't guarantee it was safe
- Other pilots' flights may be beyond your skill level
- Always assess your own risk
- Don't attempt flights beyond your ability

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I undo sharing a flight?

**Secret links:** Revoke the link from **Shared links** in the Account menu (or delete the flight).
**Public profile:** Disable public profile to hide all flights.

### Can I share only some flights to the community?

Not currently. Public profile makes all flights public. Use secret links for selective sharing.

### Will my flights show up in Google search?

Community pages are only visible to signed-in pilots and are never indexed. Secret links are not listed anywhere by We-Fly, but if you post one on a public web page, a search engine can find it like any other link.

### Can I download other pilots' IGC files?

Yes.

### Can I see who viewed my shared flights?

Not currently.

### What happens if I delete my account?

All your flights, secret links, and public data are permanently removed.
