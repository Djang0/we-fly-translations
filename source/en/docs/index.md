## What's We-Fly All About?

Your paragliding logbook in the cloud. Upload your IGC files, get instant flight stats, thermal breakdowns, and performance tracking. No more "I swear I climbed at 4m/s" debates at the LZ. The data doesn't lie.

Track your progress, chase triangles, and finally prove you're not always the first one on the retrieve truck. Blue skies!

---

## Core Features

### Analytics & Performance

**[Cockpit Analytics](/doc/cockpit)**
Your flight stats dashboard: trends, performance, and the numbers that matter. Track your progression from thermalling newbie to XC machine.

**[Advanced Thermal Analysis](/doc/advanced-thermal-analysis)**
Deep dive into your thermal performance: find out which climbs were money and which were garbage. Every turn analyzed, every vario beep decoded.

**[How Thermal Detection Works](/doc/advanced-thermal-analysis-settings)**
The nitty-gritty of thermal detection: which signals the analysis reads, the thresholds it uses, and why a climb does or doesn't count as a thermal.

### Data & Validation

**[Data Filters](/doc/data-filters)**
Slice and dice your data: analyze specific date ranges, sites, or conditions. Compare your valley floor grinds to your epic cloudbase days.

**[FAI Validation](/doc/fai-validation)**
Validate your flights with FAI's official API: make those badges official. No more screenshots of XC Tracer, just pure verified glory.

**[Get Your Data](/doc/get-your-data)**
Export your complete flight archive. Because it's YOUR data. Take it with you, back it up, or feed it to your own analysis tools.

### Community & Sharing

**[Community & Sharing](/doc/community)**
Share your epic flights and connect with fellow sky junkies. Show off that 100km triangle or commiserate about that one thermal you should've left.
